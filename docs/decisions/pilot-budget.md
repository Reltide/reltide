# MAX-11 — pilot deployment and budget

**Decision date:** 2026-09-25. **Target:** below €100 per month for a small, low-traffic pilot, including a modeled Finnish 25.5% VAT charge. This is a planning envelope, conditional on the pre-deployment checks below, not a provider quote or a promise of availability. Prices and plan terms were checked on the decision date.

## Monthly envelope

Use a 31-day **744-hour** month for the always-on VM; no credits, reserved discounts, or promotional free compute. Convert USD charges at **$1 = €1** for the budget, a planning buffer rather than an exchange-rate quote. Actual card conversion, payment fees, and tax depend on the billing entity. Neon assumptions are **120 active hours at 0.25 CU**, 1 GB-month application data, 1 GB-month retained history changes, and 1 GB-month scheduled snapshot storage. R2 assumptions are at most 10 GB-month and well within the free operation quotas. The €2 R2 allowance covers small overages and rounding; it is not a quoted charge.

| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Verda base CPU | 744 h × €0.0413 | 30.73 |
| Verda OS + Temporal PostgreSQL volumes | 100 GiB × €0.1723 | 17.23 |
| Verda registry | 2 GiB × €0.1723 | 0.34 |
| Neon Launch | 120 h × 0.25 CU × $0.106 + $0.35 + $0.20 + $0.09 | 3.82 |
| R2 artifact/backup overage allowance | 10 GB-month free assumed; extra reserved | 2.00 |
| Resend Free | Below 3,000/month and 100/day | 0.00 |

The VAT row models **nonrecoverable Finnish VAT**: Finland's general rate is 25.5%; reverse-charge and deductibility depend on the purchaser and supplier. Use the actual invoices and billing country before approval. [Finnish Tax Administration rate](https://www.vero.fi/en/businesses-and-corporations/taxes-and-charges/vat/rates-of-vat/), [cross-border services](https://www.vero.fi/en/businesses-and-corporations/taxes-and-charges/vat/international-commerce/vat-on-international-supply-of-services/).

Resend Free and the previous $0 email assumption have the same arithmetic, so the modeled **€93.02** total is unchanged. At the planning FX and VAT assumptions, Resend Pro would add **€20 × 1.255 = €25.10**, taking the three-run total to **€118.12**. Reaching the Free limit therefore stops new email-generating work until the budget is revised; there is no automatic paid upgrade. [Resend pricing](https://resend.com/pricing).

The 120-hour Neon assumption is material: at 0.25 CU for all 744 hours, the same Neon line becomes **$20.356**, and the modeled total with three jobs becomes **€113.78 including VAT**. Scale to zero, no keepalive query, a 0.25 CU maximum, weekly usage review, and suspension of *new jobs* when the forecast crosses the cap are required. Neon paid plans send spending notifications, but notifications are not a hard monthly limit. [Neon pricing](https://neon.com/pricing).

## Admission and per-run caps

Admit **at most three runs/month**, **one concurrent run**, only when `already billed + committed fixed costs + €8 recovery reserve + remaining admitted job caps`, grossed up by the planning VAT factor, stays below **€95**. The €5 difference to the target absorbs billing and FX movement. A failed or cancelled run still consumes its actual spend; it does not grant a new free slot.

| Guardrail per admitted run | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Total incremental spending | **€4.00** at the planning FX rate: AI ≤€2.50, disposable VM and OS disk ≤€0.50, R2 churn ≤€0.25, unallocated variance ≤€0.75. These are additive categories. |
| Model | Across the original attempt, verification, and one retry: ≤100,000 billable input tokens, ≤20,000 billable output tokens, and ≤€2.50 projected cost; stop at the first limit. Reserve ≤€0.40 of that model cap for verification and ≤€0.60 for retry. The selected provider's maximum possible in-flight output must fit the remaining balance before each request. |
| VM and elapsed time | One disposable on-demand CPU VM; ≤4 billable VM hours and ≤4 hours end-to-end. A retry may use at most 2 of those hours. At public rates, 4 h × €0.0413 + 50 GiB × €0.1723 × 4/744 ≈ €0.212; the €0.50 cap still requires a FIN-location quote. |
| Verification and retry | One bounded verification cycle, ≤30 VM minutes and the ≤€0.40 model subcap; at most one retry, with its own ≤€0.60 model and ≤2 VM-hour subcaps. These are **inside**, not in addition to, the model and VM totals. |
| Artifacts and cleanup | ≤1 GiB new retained artifacts per run, ≤10,000 R2 Class A and ≤100,000 Class B operations; expire artifacts to keep the account within 10 GB-month. Shut down and delete the disposable VM and its OS volume within 10 minutes of finish/cancel/timeout; confirm both are gone before admitting another job. No retained disposable disk is allowed. |

AI price sensitivity before choosing a provider: if its published rates are `p_in` and `p_out` USD per million tokens, the run's maximum token quantities would cost `0.10 × p_in + 0.02 × p_out` euros at the planning FX rate, before any cache, tool, or request fees. Each $1/million rise in the input rate adds €0.10 at that ceiling; each $1/million rise in output adds €0.02. The selected model must fit the **€2.50 total AI cap including verification and retry** at its published rate, or the token/attempt limits must be lowered before the pilot.

Provider billing may lag local metering. A hard financial guarantee needs prepaid/provider limits or an adapter that reserves the worst-case cost of each call before dispatch. Until tested, these are **operational admission rules**, and a run cannot claim an enforced €4 ceiling. Verda uses prepaid 10-minute billing; if its project balance reaches zero, instances and volumes can be deleted, so maintain at least 48 hours of base-service balance and a low-balance alert. [Verda billing](https://docs.verda.com/welcome-to-verda/pricing-and-billing/).

## Backups, recovery, and deployment gates

- **Temporal PostgreSQL:** make an encrypted whole-cluster backup to a separate R2 EU-jurisdiction backup bucket daily; retain a bounded set so backup objects average ≤5 GB-month. Verify backup success daily and restore to a disposable Verda VM monthly. Planning RPO is up to 24 hours after a successful backup; RTO is unmeasured until the first restore drill. The €8 recovery reserve cannot pay for a long replacement deployment. The single VM and data volume are one failure domain.
- **Neon application DB:** choose a 7-day restore window and one scheduled snapshot. The table budgets 1 GB-month of history changes and 1 GB-month of snapshot data; usage above these assumptions consumes headroom. Verify an actual restore before accepting important pilot data. [Neon pricing](https://neon.com/pricing).
- **Before deployment:** obtain an authenticated Verda `FIN-01` availability and cost estimate for both 4-vCPU VMs, OS volumes, data volume, and registry; check OS image and quota. The CLI is installed but had **no authenticated profile** on 2026-09-25, so no location-specific quote or resource was obtained. Load-test whether 4 vCPU/16 GB can host the app, worker, target Temporal v1.31.3, and target PostgreSQL 16.15 together. With the matching Temporal schema tools, initialize core and visibility schemas, start and complete a workflow, inspect visibility, then back up and restore; the vendor sample establishes PostgreSQL **16** with Temporal **v1.31.0**, while this exact patch pairing remains unverified until that test. Confirm R2 EU jurisdiction and account-wide free-tier eligibility, Neon region/plan and billable restore configuration, Resend Free account limits and US data-transfer terms, actual VAT/FX, domain cost, and any Verda internet-egress charge. Reject new runs if projected account-wide R2 operations cross the free tier: crossing a rounded Class A billing unit can exceed the €2 R2 allowance. If any line exceeds its allowance or a paid email plan is needed, recalculate before admitting a job.
- **Before billable AI work:** select and record the provider, exact model identifier/version, input/output/cache/tool prices, data residency/retention terms, and a tested worst-case preflight spend limiter. Neither AI use nor the disposable job VM is provisioned by this decision.

This is a **single-node pilot** with manual recovery. It makes a conditional sub-€100 operating case under the stated low-traffic assumptions; it does not establish a production availability target.
